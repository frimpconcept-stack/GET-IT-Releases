# Third-party downloader engines

GET IT! is a front-end/integration. Its optional engines are independent projects and retain their own copyright, licenses, update policies and supported platforms. The automatic Windows build provisioner downloads assets from their respective official release repositories. It is the responsibility of anyone redistributing a built installer to comply with the bundled projects' license terms, including preserving required license and source-code notices.

- **aria2:** https://github.com/aria2/aria2 (GNU GPL v2 or later)
- **gallery-dl:** https://codeberg.org/mikf/gallery-dl and maintainer's binaries at https://github.com/gdl-org/builds (GNU GPL v2)
- **Streamlink:** https://github.com/streamlink/streamlink and portable Windows builds at https://github.com/streamlink/windows-builds (BSD 2-Clause license)
- **yt-dlp:** https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp (see repository LICENSE for applicable terms)
- **FFmpeg:** https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html (licensing depends on the actual FFmpeg build and compile flags)

Executable hashes validate the retrieved artifacts, but are not proof of safety. Scan downloaded files using your usual security software. Do not disable Defender/SmartScreen. Avoid downloading or redistributing media without authorization or bypassing DRM.
